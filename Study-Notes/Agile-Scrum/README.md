Agile and Scrum Notes

Agile
Agile is an iterative way of delivering software, with frequent feedback and adaptation.

Scrum Roles
- Product Owner: prioritizes the product backlog and clarifies value.
- Scrum Master: supports the Scrum process and helps remove impediments.
- Developers: build and deliver the product increment. QA may be part of this cross-functional team.

Common Events
- Sprint Planning: agree on the sprint goal and selected work.
- Daily Scrum: inspect progress and plan the next workday.
- Sprint Review: inspect the increment and gather feedback.
- Sprint Retrospective: identify ways to improve the team's process.

QA in a Sprint
QA can review acceptance criteria, identify test scenarios, prepare test data, test completed stories, report defects, retest fixes, and participate in regression testing. Testing should be considered throughout the sprint, not only at the end.
